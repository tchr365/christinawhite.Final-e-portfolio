Digital Animation Project

DIGITAL ANIMATION

I created this 15-second animation on heat transfer using Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash. I used ChatGPT initially to generate some topic ideas appropriate for middle school and went with the heat transfer idea it suggested. ChatGPT also created the storyboard and script for me. I incorporated the project's requirements into the prompt and attached the storyboard and script document generated by ChatGPT, directing Gemini to use it to help create the animation. My generated animation includes an appropriate amount of text, two or more colors and images, motion tweening, fade effects, and frame interpolation for a smooth animation. It also features a voice-over component to explain the heat-transfer process occurring in the animation. There was no need to use a traditional editor in this project. I learned that it is important to understand the capabilities of the AI tools that you are using and that when they are used together, they can make the creation process much more efficient.
